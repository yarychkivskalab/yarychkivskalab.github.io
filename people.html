Principal investigator

Olya Yarychkivska

Assistant Professor

Institute for Cancer Research

Feinstein Institutes for Medical Research / Northwell Health

Ph.D., Columbia University, 2017

Olya is originally from Ukraine and moved to the US to pursue her undergraduate education at Manor College and Drexel University. She then completed her Ph.D. in the laboratory of Dr. Timothy Bestor in the Department of Genetics and Development at Columbia University, where she studied DNA methylation and chromatin in mammalian systems.

In 2019, she joined the laboratory of Dr. Shai Shaham at The Rockefeller University as a postdoctoral fellow to study linker cell-type death (LCD), a novel cell death program in C. elegans. Olya received an F32 postdoctoral fellowship and a K99/R00 Pathway to Independence Award from NIGMS/NIH, as well as support from the Kellen Women’s Entrepreneurship Fund at The Rockefeller University.

Olya started her laboratory at the Feinstein Institutes for Medical Research at Northwell Health in 2026.
